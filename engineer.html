Duty Location Consent

TSPL Field Service Tracking

TSPL ServiceDesk records your location only between the time you punch in and the time you punch out. It is never recorded on your off days or after you punch out.

This is used to confirm the exact time you reached each ATM, to send you the calls nearest to you, and to protect you when a bank questions how long a repair took. Your route is kept for 12 months and then deleted.

You can see your own route at any time from the View My Route button on this screen. Your manager can see your location during duty hours.

Fix Location Sharing

Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet):
  1. Open phone Settings > Apps > Chrome.
  2. Tap Permissions > Location.
  3. Select Allow only while using the app (or Always).
  4. Ensure Use precise location is enabled.
Apple iPhone (Safari):
  1. Open Settings > Privacy & Security.
  2. Tap Location Services (turn ON).
  3. Scroll to Safari Websites > select While Using the App.
  4. Toggle ON Precise Location.
